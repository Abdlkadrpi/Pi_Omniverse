Privacy Policy — Omniverse

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Application Name: Omniverse

1. Overview

Omniverse provides smart city workflows, digital notarization tools, and human-supervised AI agent orchestration integrated with the Pi Network ecosystem. We prioritize data minimization, zero-trust security, and strict human oversight.

2. Information We Process

  • Pi Authentication & Ecosystem Data: When you sign in via Pi SDK / Pi Browser, we process only the authentication and payment verification data necessary to operate your session and confirm transactions.
  • Application & Utility Metering (LYO): In-app utility usage is metered using LYO (fixed maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 LYO strictly for in-app utility, with no external speculative promise).
  • User-Submitted Inputs & Audit Logs: Task inputs submitted to AI agents and security/audit records are handled under access controls and human-in-the-loop approval policies.

3. What We Do Not Store or Expose

Omniverse never requests or stores your Pi wallet passphrase or private keys. Secrets and environment credentials are isolated outside source code repositories.

4. PiNet & External Access

Certain public views or entry points may be accessible through PiNet or web interfaces subject to rate limiting, Web Application Firewall (WAF) protection, and Pi Network policies.

5. Data Retention & Contact

Operational records are retained only as needed for security auditing, reliability, and service integrity. For privacy or compliance inquiries related to Omniverse, contact the platform operator through official developer channels.
